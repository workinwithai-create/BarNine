# BarNine

Bar-9 change desk for bedroom producers and AI-music finishers.

Suno loops. DAWs stall on the same eight bars. BarNine sits on the join: pick what happens when the loop would repeat, hear Loop 8 vs Through 16, export a punch list into the DAW.

Distinct from [HookGrid](https://github.com/workinwithai-create/HookGrid) (song maps), [MuteEight](https://github.com/workinwithai-create/MuteEight) (subtraction / density), and [RefPunch](https://github.com/workinwithai-create/RefPunch) (mix-vs-reference notes). This is the event at bar 9 — not form, not mute, not loudness.

## Loop

1. Pick a groove (Night House, Pop Radio, Trap Topline, Indie Ballad, D&B Roller).
2. Hear the eight-bar loop. This is the hate.
3. Pick one bar-9 move: crash the 1, snare run, kick flip, hats cut, bass invert, half-time, air then slam, lead enter.
4. Switch to Through 16. Bar 9 fires. Bars 10–16 keep the new state.
5. Copy the punch list into the DAW.

Space plays. `1` loops, `2` plays through. Audio never leaves the tab.
